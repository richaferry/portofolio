FAQ

Questions You Might Have

Answers to the things clients usually ask before we start working together.

What's your typical rate?
Depends on the engagement model. Project-based work is scoped and quoted upfront. For ongoing or full-time contracts, I work on a competitive rate that reflects 10+ years of production experience. Let's talk and I'll give you a transparent quote — no surprises.
How do you handle timezone differences?
I'm based in Indonesia (WIB / GMT+7) but I'm experienced working across time zones — from US Pacific to EU Central. I default to async communication (detailed PR descriptions, Loom videos, Slack/email) with scheduled overlap for important syncs. I've been doing this for years with distributed teams.
What's your availability right now?
I'm currently open to new projects and can typically start within 1-2 weeks depending on scope. For urgent needs, reach out and I'll tell you honestly if I can accommodate your timeline.
Do you work solo or can you bring a team?
I primarily work as an individual contributor — which means you get a single, accountable engineer who owns the result. For projects that need more hands, I can tap into a trusted network of designers and developers I've worked with before.
What's your preferred tech stack?
I'm most productive with TypeScript/Node.js on the backend and React/Next.js on the frontend, deployed on Cloudflare Workers or Docker. That said, I'm pragmatic — I've shipped production PHP (Laravel, WordPress), Python, and various databases. I pick the right tool for the job, not the shiniest one.
